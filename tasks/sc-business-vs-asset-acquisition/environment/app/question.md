# Question — business or asset acquisition

Acquisition: **The acquirer buys several vacant land parcels from a developer. Nothing else is transferred.**

Using `/app/policy.md`, what was acquired? Select exactly one option.

- A. A business
- B. An asset (or group of assets), not a business

## Answer format

Write `/app/answer.json` as a JSON object, for example:

```json
{"final_answer": "A", "confidence": 80}
```

`confidence` is your self-assessed probability (0-100) that the answer
is fully correct.
