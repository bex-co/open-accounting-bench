# Question — capitalization treatment

On 2026-01-12 the company bought a laptop from Lenovo for USD 5025.00,
paid from checking. The laptop has a useful life of 5 years.
Accounting policy: see `/app/policy.md` (items with a useful life over
one year are capitalized; consumables are expensed).

Select **all** statements that correctly describe the required
accounting treatment on the purchase date.

- A. Debit Assets:Bank:Checking 5025.00 USD and credit Income:Sales 5025.00 USD on the purchase date.
- B. Debit Expenses:Repairs 5025.00 USD and credit Assets:Bank:Checking 5025.00 USD, expensing the full cost immediately.
- C. The purchase does not reduce profit on the acquisition date; cost is recognized over the useful life.
- D. Debit Assets:Equipment 5025.00 USD and credit Expenses:Repairs 5025.00 USD, with no cash movement on the purchase date.
- E. Debit Assets:Equipment 5025.00 USD and credit Assets:Bank:Checking 5025.00 USD on the purchase date.

## Answer format

Write `/app/answer.json` as a JSON object, for example:

```json
{"final_answer": ["A", "C"], "confidence": 80}
```

`final_answer` lists the selected option ids; `confidence` is your
self-assessed probability (0-100) that the selection is fully correct.
Incorrect selections are penalized, so select only statements you
believe are correct.
