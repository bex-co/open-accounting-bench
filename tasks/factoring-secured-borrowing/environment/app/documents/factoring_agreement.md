# Factoring agreement — 2026-01-12

The company pledged receivables of USD 108000.00 to a factor, which advanced 80% (USD 86400.00) less a 3% fee on the advance (USD 2592.00), paying USD 83808.00 into checking. Legal title to the receivables was not transferred: the factor has only a security interest, cannot sell or pledge them, and the company retains the risks of uncollectible accounts and control of collections. Customers were not notified.
