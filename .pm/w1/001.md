# Gradable-scope records and a second coverage denominator

`docs/metrics.md#framework-coverage` admits the gap: coverage
denominators count every blueprint/syllabus item, including ones no
deterministic grader can check (purpose recall, disclosure prose).

- Add `[[out_of_scope]]` records to `crosswalks/<edition>.toml`:
  `target_id`, `target_item` (AICPA), OAB-written `reason`, `status`,
  `reviewer`. Record exclusions only — absent items stay in scope.
- `oab.crosswalk`: validate them (prefix in denominators, unique,
  reason required, reviewed ⇒ reviewer, never both out of scope and
  covered); report `Gradable = Items − out_of_scope` beside `Items`;
  count unreviewed exclusions in `Unreviewed`.
- `docs/metrics.md`: cite both denominators; the gradable form only
  once its exclusions are reviewed.
- Seed data from gradability triage: AICPA 2027 FAR 10 exclusions
  (gradable 103 of 113); ACCA 2026-27 FA 69 exclusions (85 of 154).
  Records arrive with the triage review; ship schema + tests first.
