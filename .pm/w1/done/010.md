(as delivered: instruction + policy.md + documents):

`classify-operating-expense` (vocational) matches. Accounting review
(2026-10-23, Tian Pan, maintainer) confirmed the four rows above and
found three more scores lenient:

| Task | Lenient | Strict | Lenient-only scores |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bank-recon-missing-fee` | university (borderline) | vocational | dependent steps U (both amounts read off the statement, target balance given); evidence U (no real distractor) |
| `mc-capitalization-treatment` | university (borderline) | vocational | scope U (one purchase-date transaction; option C only judges a multi-period statement) |
| `ifrs-capitalize-borrowing-costs` | university | vocational or university | evidence U (one clean document); judgement U ("substantial period" fixed by policy and dates) |

Under the strict reading every Stable task is vocational. No task
scores P on any dimension: OAB has no genuinely professional task yet,
mainly because policy.md hands over the decisive rule and instructions
name the problem.

Decide per task: **relabel** (metadata only — regenerate tasks, no
`GENERATOR_VERSION` bump per docs/governance.md) or **harden** the
generator so it earns the current label (e.g. derive the period amount,
find the misposting among several entries, withhold the formula) —
that changes expectations and does bump the version. Needs accounting
review either way; the lenient-vs-strict reading is part of that
decision. Leaderboard `By level` groups are misleading until
resolved.

**Blocked 2026-10-23** — the fix is a maintainer decision, not something evidence can settle: (1) relabel (metadata only, no `GENERATOR_VERSION` bump) or harden the generators (changes expectations, bumps the version), and (2) the lenient or strict reading for `bank-recon-missing-fee`, `mc-capitalization-treatment`, and `ifrs-capitalize-borrowing-costs`.
**Unblock:** the maintainer records the choice for each of the 7 tasks (relabel to which level, or harden how). Who: a maintainer with accounting background. New families on `family/*` branches already carry rubric-based levels.
