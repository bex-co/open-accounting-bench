## Objective

Found by the m10 `/simplify` altitude review: `CLOSE_EXTRA_ACCOUNTS`
repeats `Liabilities:AccruedWages` and `Expenses:Wages`, which the
evidence pack already opens, so every month-end close ledger opens them
twice (real Beancount rejects duplicate opens). Remove the overlap at the
source and guard against it.

## Context

- `src/oab/workflow_families.py`: `CLOSE_EXTRA_ACCOUNTS`, the `opens`
  list built from it, and `_close_chart()` (whose dedupe only hid the
  problem in the chart).
- `src/oab/evidence.py`: `EXTRA_ACCOUNTS` (opened by the pack ledger).

## Steps

1. Add a failing test: no account is opened twice in a close ledger.
2. Drop the two overlapping entries; assert the two account lists are disjoint.
3. Decide the `GENERATOR_VERSION` bump; `make generate`; run all gates.
