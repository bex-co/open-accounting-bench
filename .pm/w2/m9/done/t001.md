## Objective

Specify at least four chains where one correction feeds another (returned check reinstates a receivable that enters the aging and the allowance; impairment changes later depreciation; cutoff reversal changes inventory and the write-down base; accrual changes the reported total expenses).

## Context

- APEX: models drop or contradict correct intermediate results downstream; FinancialAuditBench: 17% fail to consider effects of changes.

## Steps

1. Write each chain with the downstream check that catches a dropped link.

## Design (2026-11-07)

Chains already implied by the generator's expectations, plus one gap to
close. Each row names the first link, the dependent computation, the
mutant that applies only the first link, and the determination whose
check must catch it.

| # | First link | Downstream effect | First-link-only mutant | Caught by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue cutoff reverses the premature sale | The reversed receivable leaves the allowance base | Reverse the sale but still reserve the premature receivable (1%, not yet due) | `receivables-allowance` primary delta |
| 2 | Returned check reinstates the receivable | The reinstated row enters the aging at 20% (over 30 days) | Record the returned check but leave it out of the allowance | `receivables-allowance` primary delta |
| 3 | Principal paid on the 15th | Interest on the average of opening and ending principal | Accrue interest on the opening principal | `debt-interest-accrual` primary delta |
| 4 | Each close adjustment | Current-period total expenses and operating income | Post every adjustment but report the draft totals | `reporting` total-expenses / operating-income checks |
| 5 | Routine repair expensed out of equipment | Its January depreciation is reversed | Expense the repair but keep the depreciation | **gap:** no `fixed-asset-treatment` check covers depreciation; t002 adds a depreciation-expense delta check to that determination |
