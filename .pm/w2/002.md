# Declared plausibility invariants for generator seed sweeps

Accounting reviews keep finding seeds that are valid but unrealistic
(implied prices inconsistent across stakes, negative adjusted basis,
bundled price far from standalone prices). Let a generator declare
plausibility invariants (ratios, sign constraints, bounds) and have the
1,000-seed sweep in `tests/test_generators.py` check them.

- Bounds come from textbook and public practice conventions only — no
  real records (`.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`, PROVENANCE.md).
- Start with the scaffold plus three families; promote to a milestone
  if rolling it out across all families is wanted.
- Pillar B1. About 1h for the scaffold.
