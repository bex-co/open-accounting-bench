## Objective

Found by the w2/m1 `/simplify` altitude review: currency constraints are
read from the final ledger, so an agent can rewrite an initial
`open Account USD` line to drop the constraint and then post foreign
currency to it. Fix the root cause in the verifier core, and fold the
two layer-5 "unauthorized change" loops into one rule.

## Context

- `src/oab/_verifier_core.py`: `open_currencies` is taken from `final`;
  layer 5 has a reporting-currency loop (tolerance) and a separate
  non-reporting-currency loop (exact, final keys only).
- `src/oab/audit.py`: add a false-accept operator for an edited `open`
  directive so the audit covers the bypass.
- Also share the grader's `expected_new_transactions` bounds and numeric
  tolerance formula with `audit.py` (helpers in the core) so probes
  cannot drift from what the grader checks.

## Steps

1. Add the audit operator and a failing verifier test; confirm both fail.
2. Enforce initial `open` constraints and flag altered or removed
   initial `open` directives under operational integrity.
3. One integrity loop over every (account, currency) key in initial and
   final balances, with one tolerance rule.
4. Extract the count-bounds and tolerance helpers; use them in the core
   and in `audit.py`.
5. Decide the `GENERATOR_VERSION` bump, `make generate`, run all gates.
